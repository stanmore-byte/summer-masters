01

Change Direction

Build your footwork foundation — sharp cuts, quick stops

0%
done
Monday
⚡ Footwork Basics
DRILL #1
The Freeze
Stand still. Lean your whole body hard left — then hard right. Feel your weight shift onto each foot. 10 reps each side. Master your balance first.
DRILL #2
The Sway Walk
Walk forward and exaggerate a big hip lean left then right every 2 steps. Big movements! 3 sets of 15m.
DRILL #3
Zigzag Walk
Set up 5 cones or shoes 1.5m apart. Walk through them, touching each one. Focus on angle — go past each, not just around. 3 sets.
DRILL #4
Zigzag Jog
Same 5 cones as Drill 3 — now jog! Sharp angles at each cone. 3 sets. Don't advance until your angles look crisp.
Tuesday
⚽ Ball Intro
DRILL #5
Dribble Straight
Dribble the ball in a straight line — alternate inside and outside of your foot. Keep the ball close. 3 sets of 20m each way.
DRILL #6
Stop and Go
Dribble 5 steps, trap the ball dead with your sole, then explode forward again. 5 sets. The stop has to be clean — no rolling!
DRILL #7
Slow Zigzag with Ball
Same 5 cone setup as before — now dribble through them slowly. Keep the ball under control. 3 sets. You're the boss of the ball!
Wednesday
🔪 Sharp Cuts
DRILL #8
Speed Zigzag
Back to the 5 cones — now faster with the ball! Push yourself. 3 sets. Rest 1 minute between sets.
DRILL #9
The L-Cut
Dribble straight at a cone, then plant your outside foot hard and cut 90° to one side using the inside of your other foot. 5 reps each side.
DRILL #10
The V-Cut
Dribble to a cone, stop ball with your sole, then push it back the direction you came (but slightly to one side). Like drawing a V. 5 reps each side.
Thursday
💚 Recovery Day
🧘 Active Recovery — 10 to 30 Minutes

Light jog or stretch · Juggling practice · Watch a Messi or Neymar body feint highlight on YouTube · Rest your legs but keep the ball at your feet.

Friday
💥 Accelerate Out
DRILL #11
The Sharp Cut
Sprint hard at a cone, plant, cut 90° — then immediately sprint 5m in the new direction. 5 reps each side. The burst after the cut is KEY.
DRILL #12
The Double Cut
Cut left, sprint 3 steps, then immediately cut right. Back and forth between 2 cones. 5 reps. This trains your body to change direction quickly twice.
DRILL #13
Cut & Accelerate
Every cut in this drill is followed by a 5-step full-speed burst. If you're not tired after this one, you're not going hard enough! 5 reps each side.
Saturday
🏆 Full Speed Test
DRILL #14
Full Speed V-Cut
Max speed V-cut — dribble fast, stop dead, push hard in new direction. Can someone watch and see if they can tell which way you'll go? 5 reps each side.
DRILL #15
Direction Medley
Mix it up! L-cut, V-cut, double cut — pick 3 from this week and do them back to back. Full speed. 10 reps. This is your Week 1 TEST. You've earned it! 🏅
Sunday
😴 Rest Day
Complete rest — your muscles grow while you sleep. See you Monday! 💤
🏅 WEEK 1 COMPLETE — You're a Direction Master! Week 2 Unlocked! 🏅
02

Basic Body Feint & Double Fake

The real move — fool defenders with your body, not your feet

0%
done
Monday
🎭 The Feint — No Ball
DRILL #16
The Shoulder Drop
Stand still. Drop your right shoulder hard — like you're diving right — but stay put. Then left shoulder. Really sell it! 10 each side. Look in a mirror if you can.
DRILL #17
Walking Feint
Walk forward. Every 3 steps, do a big shoulder drop to one side. Your shoulders and hips both go — then keep walking straight. 3 sets of 15m.
DRILL #18
Jogging Feint
Same as Drill 17 but jogging! Feint every 4–5 steps. It will feel weird at first — that's normal! 3 sets of 20m.
Tuesday
⚽ Feint With Ball
DRILL #19
Feint & Go (Walking)
Walk with the ball. Drop your shoulder LEFT — then push the ball RIGHT using the outside of your left foot. Slow and smooth. 5 reps each way. THIS is the body feint!
DRILL #20
Feint Through Cones
Set up 5 cones. At EACH cone, do the shoulder drop body feint to get past it. Walking pace. Focus on making it look real. 3 sets.
DRILL #21
Feint & Burst
After every feint, take 3 explosive accelerating steps. Feint → burst → stop → feint → burst. 5 reps each side. The acceleration makes defenders think they've already lost you.
Wednesday
🚀 Feint at Speed
DRILL #22
Jog Feint
Dribble at jogging pace, drop shoulder, push ball the other way. The move should start to feel natural now. 3 sets of 5 reps each side.
DRILL #23
Feint at Speed
Sprint toward a cone, do the body feint 2 steps before it, then accelerate in the new direction. 5 reps each side. Speed makes the feint work in real games!
DRILL #24
1v Cone Defender
Put one cone as your pretend defender. Dribble toward it. Use your body feint to beat it and go past. 10 reps. Imagine a real player there!
Thursday
💚 Recovery Day
🧘 Active Recovery — 10 to 30 Minutes

Light juggling · Ball touches against a wall · Film yourself doing the body feint and watch it back — can you see it working? · Rest those legs.

Friday
🃏 The Double Fake
DRILL #25
Double Feint — No Ball
Feint LEFT with your shoulder… then immediately feint RIGHT… then explode left! Two fakes in one move. Standing still first. 10 reps each direction. This is DEADLY.
DRILL #26
Double Fake with Ball
Walk with ball, do the double fake, then push ball in final direction. Feint left, feint right, go left. 5 reps each way. You're becoming dangerous!
Saturday
⚡ Double Fake Speed
DRILL #27
Double Fake Speed Run
Full speed double fake through a line of 3 cones. At each one: feint, feint, go. 3 sets of 5 reps. Film yourself — you'll be shocked how good it looks. Week 2 DONE! 🏅
Sunday
😴 Rest Day
Rest up — Week 3 is the one where it all comes together. You've got this. 💤
🔮 WEEK 2 COMPLETE — You're a Feint Apprentice! One week to MASTERY! 🔮
03

Mix & Combination

Put it all together — game speed, real pressure, full mastery

0%
done
Monday
🎯 Feint into Action
DRILL #28
Feint to Shoot
Dribble to a cone (your pretend defender), do a body feint to beat it, then hit a shot at the goal (or target on a wall). 10 reps. Feint the keeper, score the goal!
DRILL #29
Feint to Pass
Feint past your cone defender, then pass the ball accurately to a target (a wall, another cone, or a parent). 10 reps. In real games, the feint creates the pass lane!
DRILL #30
Feint to Sprint
Feint past cone, then full speed sprint for 10m. The sprint HAS to happen immediately — no hesitation! 10 reps. This is how you beat defenders in a real match.
Tuesday
🧱 Cone Gauntlet
DRILL #31
1v1 vs Cone
Set up 1 cone 5m in front of you. Dribble at it, use ANY feint you've learned to beat it, and accelerate past. 10 reps — try different moves each time!
DRILL #32
3-Cone Gauntlet
3 cones in a row, 3m apart. You must beat EACH ONE with a body feint. Different feint at each cone! 5 runs through. You're a nightmare for defenders.
DRILL #33
Counter Attack Run
Start from your own half. Receive ball, beat a cone with a feint, sprint to goal, shoot. Do it in under 5 seconds! 10 reps. This is how goals happen in real games.
Wednesday
🔀 Mix & Combine
DRILL #34
Feint + Pull Back
Combine: body feint left → pull ball back with sole → push right. Three moves in one! Walk through it first, then slowly faster. 5 reps each side.
DRILL #35
Double Fake + V-Cut
Do a double fake → then finish with a V-cut in the final direction. You've got the defender guessing TWICE before you cut away. 5 reps each side.
DRILL #36
Combination Rush
Pick ANY 2 moves from the whole program. Do them back-to-back at full speed. 10 reps. Try a new combination each time — there are no wrong answers!
Thursday
💚 Recovery Day
🧘 Active Recovery — 10 to 30 Minutes

Light juggling · Review your favourite drill from Week 1 and Week 2 — can you do them even better now? · Think about which move feels most natural to you. That's your signature move!

Friday
🆚 Real Pressure
DRILL #37
The Guessing Game
Ask a parent or sibling to stand in front of you like a defender. Do your feint — can they guess which way you'll go? If they can't, you've mastered it. 10 rounds each.
DRILL #38
Keep Away Box
Mark out a small 4m × 4m box. One of you has the ball, one tries to win it. Ball holder uses feints to stay away. 2 minutes each — use every trick you've learned!
DRILL #39
Move of the Day
Pick your BEST feint. Film yourself doing it 10 times at full speed. Watch it back. Is it smooth? Is it fast? Is it convincing? Keep going until the answer is YES to all three.
Saturday
🏆 MASTERY DAY
DRILL #40
Full Game Speed — All Moves
Go through ALL your favourite moves at full game speed. Call out the name of the move before you do it, then execute it perfectly. 5 reps of each move. You're in complete control now.

THE SHOW — Mastery Challenge
